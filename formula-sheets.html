Network Theory Formula Sheets

Important formulas for GATE Electrical Engineering revision.

Ohm's Law

V = IR

Voltage = Current × Resistance.

Kirchhoff's Current Law

Σ I entering = Σ I leaving

Current conservation at a node.

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law

Σ V around a closed loop = 0

Voltage rises and drops sum to zero.

Thevenin's Theorem

Vth = Open-circuit voltage

Rth = Resistance seen at terminals

Norton's Theorem

In = Short-circuit current

Rn = Rth

Maximum Power Transfer

RL = Rth

Pmax = Vth² / (4Rth)

For a DC resistive network.

Capacitor

i = C (dv/dt)

W = ½CV²

Inductor

v = L (di/dt)

W = ½LI²

RC Time Constant

τ = RC

RL Time Constant

τ = L/R

Angular Frequency

ω = 2πf

Series RLC Resonance

f₀ = 1 / (2π√LC)

At resonance, inductive and capacitive reactances are equal.
